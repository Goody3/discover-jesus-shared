## Vision

The Discover Jesus Platform is being created as a tool to meet the following goal:
> Discover Jesus will be a vehicle through which all users can hear the gospel and have the opportunity to spiritually mature.

It will be built using the principal:
> Identify the culture to use the culture to reach the culture.

## Purpose and problem

### Who is the platform for?

While the platform is aimed at ALL people, it is necessary two focus on three distinct audiences:

#### The Unchurched

For the unchurched the objective is to have a clear presentation of Jesus, his life, death and resurrection.  Ultimately it should give individuals a clear path to see the good news that Christ died on their behalf.

#### The Churched

The platform should provide tools for establishing community that will lead towards individual and community spiritual growth.

#### Churches

The platform will provide tools to help orchestrate communication within church communities.  It will provide churches a platform for interacting with their church body.

## What needs, challenges or opportunities should it address?

The platform will start by meeting the needs of the Austin Ridge Embrace ministry.  The platform will be built in a fashion so that multiple churches can create custom groups and user presentations.
